Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

What DataProQ LLC collects when you use our applications, why, and what we do with it. This is the only privacy policy that applies to DataProQ LLC and its applications.

Information we collect

  • Account details — email address, mobile phone number and name, supplied when you create an account or sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Messaging consent records — the date, time and method by which you agreed to receive text messages from us, kept as proof of your consent.
  • Authentication data — sign-in times, device type and IP address, used to secure your account and to show which devices are signed in.
  • Content you create — information you enter in the app.
  • Diagnostics — crash and error reports, which may include a request identifier and device model.

Text messaging and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third party. We do not sell, rent or trade your mobile phone number or your messaging consent data.

How you opt in. You provide your mobile number when you create a DataProQ account, or when you add a number in the app's security settings. Before the number is saved you confirm that you agree to receive text messages from DataProQ. We record the date, time and method of that consent. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

What we send. We use your mobile number only to send one-time passcodes for signing in, and account and security notifications you have asked for. We do not use your mobile number for marketing or promotional messages of any kind.

Message frequency varies with your activity — typically one message per sign-in attempt or account security event. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. Opting out of messages does not delete your account.

The only company that receives your mobile number in connection with these messages is Twilio, our SMS delivery provider. Twilio transmits the message to your mobile carrier on our behalf and is contractually prohibited from using your number for any other purpose. Sharing with subcontractors who support the service in this way is limited to what is needed to deliver the messages you request, and is never for marketing or promotional purposes.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account
  • To send one-time passcodes and account notifications
  • To provide the features you use
  • To diagnose problems and improve reliability
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we never share mobile information or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We share information only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contract, solely to operate our applications, and only as much as they need:

  • Supabase — account storage and authentication
  • Google Cloud — application hosting
  • Twilio — delivering SMS passcodes
  • Apple and Google — if you choose to sign in with them

These providers act as our processors. They are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes, including marketing. We also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Delete your account and we remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it.

Your choices

  • Access, correct or delete your information in the app, or by emailing us
  • Request an export of your data
  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving them
  • Sign out other devices from the app's security settings

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your device locked.

Children

Our applications are not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
